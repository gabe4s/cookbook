# Flour Tortillas
_Makes 12 ~6in portions_

## Ingredients
- 260g Flour
- 150g Water
- 75g Lard
- 2tsp Salt

## Instructions
1. Mix flour and lard together until beady
2. Mix warm/hot water and salt
3. Add salt water to flour mix and knead
4. Wrap and rest
5. Cut into ~40g portions and roll out
6. Cook on med high skilled
