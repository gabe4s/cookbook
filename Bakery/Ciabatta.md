# Ciabatta
_Makes 1 small ~2 person loaf_

## Ingredients

### Biga
- 0.4g Red Star Platinum Instant Yeast
- 80g 00 Flour
- 70g Water
- 1g Salt

### Dough
- 1.4g Instant Yeast
- 120g 00 Flour
- 110g Water
- 4g Salt

### Extra
- Ice for steam

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Rise for 45 mins, stretch dough (4x)
3. Pour dough out onto parchment paper
4. Rest 1 hour
5. Dust with flour
6. Bake @ 450f ~25 mins
