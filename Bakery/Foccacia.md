# Focaccia
_Makes 1 bread_

## Ingredients


### Biga
- 1g Instant Yeast
- 75g 00 Flour
- 75g Water

### Dough
- 1g Instant Yeast
- 225g 00 Flour
- 210g Water
- 6g Kosher Salt
- 2tbsp Olive Oil
- Salt for topping
- Rosemary (Optional)
- Garlic (Optional)

### Extra
- Butter + Oil For Pan

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients for biga, rest overnight
2. Add oil to biga next day, mix
3. Add dough ingredients, mix
4. Rise for 30 mins, stretch dough (4x)
5. Coat pan in butter + oil
6. Pour dough out onto oiled pan
7. Rest 1 hour
8. Shape and dimple
9. Top with salt
10. Bake @ 450-500 until golden brown
